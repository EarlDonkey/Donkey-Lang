Innovative Compound Architecture

The 50/50 Hybrid Model Ecosystem

Rather than relying solely on unpredictable public donations, our compound divides operations between a commercial veterinary practice and a compassionate stray sanctuary. Revenue from pet owners directly subsidizes emergency stray care.

Revenue Engine (50%)

For-Profit Veterinary Clinic

Providing full veterinary services for community pet owners. High-quality care at fair rates generates steady, predictable profit margins reinvested straight into the compound.

  • Advanced Surgical Suite & Digital X-Ray Diagnostics
  • Routine Vaccinations, Grooming & Dental Care
  • Paid Pet Boarding & Daycare Facilities
  • 100% Net Profit Transferred to Stray Medical Fund
Client Satisfaction Target: 98% Sustains 40+ Shelter Beds Daily
Mission Focus (50%)

Non-Profit Stray Shelter & Sanctuary

Dedicated to taking in abandoned, sick, or injured stray dogs. Providing trauma care, rehabilitation, nutritious meals, and peaceful indoor/outdoor living until adoption.

  • 24/7 Stray Emergency Intake & Triage Care
  • Long-term Sanctuary Beds for Senior or Non-Adoptable Dogs
  • Behavior Rehabilitation & Reflective Walking Trails
  • Transparent Adoption & Community Education Programs
Zero Euthanasia Policy Full Medical Traceability

Milo's Journey

Adopted

Rescued from Roadside Ditch • Age ~3 Yrs • Mixed Breed

Milo was found malnourished with severe skin infection and a fractured left hind leg from a vehicle strike. Our clinic performed orthopedic surgery on Day 2, followed by 6 weeks of dedicated hydrotherapy and sanctuary walks.

Condition at Intake: Severe Malnutrition, Fractured Femur
Clinic Treatment: Bone Fixation Surgery & Antibiotics
Rehabilitation: Daily Guided Walks & High-Protein Diet
Current Status: Fully Healed & Happily Adopted

Founder's Note

A Personal Commitment to the Overlooked

"I have always found walking to be my best space for thinking and clearing my head. During my walks, I often see stray animals struggling quietly—treated as problems rather than living beings in need of care. Sympathy is easy, but meaningful action requires building real structures. Haven & Hope is my vision for a calm, professional compound where animals receive dignity, medical restoration, and long-term security."
— Henri Stoven, Project Creator Taipei / Student Developer
